Case studies

Engineering outcomes, by the numbers

Nine engagements across braking, EV drivetrains, two-wheelers, industrial safety, process improvement and machine learning.

Full hardware design and functional safety analysis for a braking system where 80% of functions are ASIL D, with heavy actuators and high-speed motor control. Delivered with optimised EMI/EMC and thermal performance, on strict deadlines, across multiple product launches.

System and software FMEA plus quantitative FTA across inverter, motor, gearbox and housing, using Ansys Medini Analyze. The safety architecture keeps a shared core with low-effort variant creation for new drive configurations.

End-to-end development of a cornering ABS for two-wheelers — requirements, hardware, AUTOSAR software, prototype build, road testing and production readiness — compliant to ISO 26262 and the GB standard.

Independent safety assessment and gap analysis for a baler machine ECU against IEC 61508:2010. Delivered a compliance report, SIL 2 verification findings and a prioritised improvement plan ahead of third-party certification.

Established a hardware engineering process meeting ASPICE 4.0 process groups and ISO 26262, through due diligence, gap analysis, tailored process documentation and a final readiness review.

DOORS scripting exports requirement metrics weekly; Power BI turns the CSVs into live dashboards synced through OneDrive — replacing manual review with real-time visibility into completeness and traceability.

A Kafka-based pipeline aggregates validation data from multiple tools and sources into one store, feeding KPI tracking, analytics, monitoring and automated reports through web, mobile and desktop dashboards.

An ML pipeline trained on weather, soiling, inverter and shading data predicts PV generation loss and degradation, using transfer learning, domain adaptation and domain generalisation to stay accurate across new sites and climates.

End-to-end cybersecurity engineering aligned to ISO/SAE 21434 and UNECE R155 — TARA, secure architecture review, secure boot and key management, penetration testing and compliance documentation, ongoing for 18 months and counting.
